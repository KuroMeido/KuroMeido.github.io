Project Audit

Scoring thresholds

Checks on naming, structure and settings are scored by the share of checked items that have a problem. A lower share scores higher. Changes apply at once and are kept in this browser.

Above the last value a check scores 0.

Good (3/3) Moderate (2/3) Critical (0 or 1/3) Not scored

Pick a project here or select its name in the table above. Select a section card to jump to it, a status to show only those checks, or any row to see its figures and advice. Each check scores 0 to 3 points on up to three axes (P Performance, S Structure, B BIM Essentials), weighted by its Impact Multiplier. Score steps are starting values and can be changed in assets/js/archicad-health.js.
